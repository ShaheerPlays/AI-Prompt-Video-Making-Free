About AI Studio

Create videos with AI

AI Studio is designed as a simple and free AI video-making platform that brings video generation, editing, voice, subtitles, images, and creative tools together in one place.

Start Creating Learn How It Works
OUR PURPOSE

Making AI video creation simpler

AI Studio brings commonly needed video-creation tools together in a single web interface. Instead of moving between several different tools, creators can work with prompts, images, voices, subtitles, projects, and editing features from one platform.

The goal is to make the creative process easier to understand and easier to use, especially for people who want to experiment with AI-powered video creation.
