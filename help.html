📚 AI Studio Help

How To Use AI Studio

Follow these steps to create your first AI video.

1. Create Account

Sign up and create your personal PIN to protect your account.

2. Create Video

Open Text To Video or Image To Video and describe your idea.

3. Improve With Gemini AI

Use Gemini AI to improve your prompts and create better scripts.

4. Add Voice And Subtitles

Add AI narration and automatic captions to your video.

5. Export

Choose quality settings and export your final video.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is AI Studio free?

Yes. This project is designed as a free AI video platform.

Can I create long videos?

The platform is designed to support videos up to 30 minutes.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Users can upload images for image-to-video creation.

Can I add my own voice?

Voice features can be connected with AI voice services.
